## Cross-Phase Integration

Verdict from integration checker: `gaps_found`.

### IC-01 — FIX-06 scope narrowed silently (material)
- **From:** `.planning/AUDIT.md` Phase 9 Recommendations (line 527): *"[#8] — Wrong-loop-variable check in structural_validator.py (critical missing-check). The most commonly observed recurring error in CLAUDE.md is undetectable by the structural layer."*
- **To:** `scripts/structural_validator.py`
- **Expected:** A `check_loop_variable_placement()` or equivalent, plus block-variable mapping in `label_patterns.json`.
- **Actual:** Zero matches for `loop_variable|check_loop|TPL_LOOP_ANNONCES|TPL_IF_LISTE_AVIS` in `structural_validator.py`.
- **Affected REQ-IDs:** FIX-06 (partial), AUD-04 (actionable-next-step traceability).
- **Assessment:** The per-phase verifier checked what the Phase 9 plans claimed, not what AUDIT.md prescribed. FIX-06 is marked complete but the second-priority item in its own scope was skipped without a deferral record.

### IC-02 — zh-HK orphaned after Phase 9 (material)
- **From:** `corrections/corrections_log.json` line 44 + `corrections/rules_summary.json` line 43 (zh-HK rule added by Phase 9 Plan 02).
- **To (expected downstream consumers — all missing the entry):**
  - `config/languages.json` line 30 — only `zh-TW`
  - `config/tone_guidelines.json` line 15 — `zh`, `zh-TW`, no `zh-HK`
  - `config/label_patterns.json` lines 187, 195 — `zh`, `zh-TW`, no `zh-HK`
  - `config/review_rules_compact.md` lines 7, 12, 29 — `zh`, `zh-TW`, no `zh-HK`
  - `scripts/structural_validator.py` line 664 — `'Hong-Kong': 'zh-TW'` (HK maps to Taiwan code)
- **Impact:** HK CSV rows are tagged `zh-TW` by the validator, so the skill's past-corrections lookup will never match the `zh-HK` rule. Phase 9 fixed the underscore/hyphen issue for Taiwan but the lookup is still broken for Hong Kong.
- **Affected REQ-IDs:** FIX-06, AUD-05.
- **Root cause:** AUDIT [#32] recommended underscore→hyphen normalization; it did not flag that HK has no country→code mapping of its own.

### IC-03 — Backup-before-write is text-instruction only (known, accepted)
- **Location:** `.claude/commands/review-translations.md` lines 524-532 (single-item 7b), lines 680-688 (batch 7b-batch), line 720 (rules_summary in 7d). All three occurrences confirmed.
- **Impact:** FIX-05 holds by convention, not by mechanism. Flagged under Human Verification Required item #1 in `09-VERIFICATION.md`.
- **Affected REQ-IDs:** FIX-05.
- **Assessment:** Acceptable for v1.2; should be considered for hardening in a future milestone (commit hook or Python wrapper that performs the copy).
